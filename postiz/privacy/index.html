Privacy Policy

Postiz, self-hosted by Jona Melvin Grobe · November 2026

About

This privacy policy covers Postiz, a self-hosted copy of an open-source publishing tool that I run on my own computer. I use it to post my own videos and images to my own accounts on YouTube ("Jona Melvin Grobe", @jmelving), TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn. Nobody else uses it. For my website, see jmelving.de/privacy.

Platform terms

Postiz uses the YouTube API Services. The YouTube Terms of Service apply: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

For the other platforms, their own terms and privacy policies apply as well (TikTok, Meta/Instagram, LinkedIn).

What data is used

When I connect one of my accounts through the platform's own sign-in (OAuth), Postiz receives an access token and basic account info (account ID, name, profile picture). It uses them only to upload my own content and set its caption, title, description, thumbnail, visibility and publish time. It does not access, collect or store any data about viewers, followers, commenters or other accounts.

Where it's stored

Tokens and account info are stored only in Postiz's database on my own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone other than the platform the content is posted to, and nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting and revoking

Access can be removed at any time in each platform's settings, for Google at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When I disconnect an account in Postiz or revoke access, the stored token and account info are deleted. Otherwise the data is kept only as long as the account stays connected.
