About

I run a self-hosted copy of the open-source tool Postiz on my own computer. I use it to schedule and post my own videos and images to my own accounts on YouTube ("Jona Melvin Grobe", @jmelving), TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn. Nobody else uses it, and it isn't offered to anyone.

Platform terms

Postiz uses the YouTube API Services. The YouTube Terms of Service apply: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
Google's Privacy Policy applies as well: https://policies.google.com/privacy

For TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn, their own terms of service apply.

What it does with my accounts

It uploads my own content and sets its caption, title, description, thumbnail, visibility and publish time. Access goes through each platform's normal sign-in (OAuth). It doesn't read or store anything from other accounts, followers or viewers.

Data

The login tokens stay on my computer and aren't shared. More in the privacy policy: https://jmelving.de/postiz/privacy/

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in each platform's settings, for Google at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the account is disconnected and its token is deleted.
