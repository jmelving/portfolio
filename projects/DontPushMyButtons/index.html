01 — Project Description

Overview

Don't Push My Buttons is an experimental conceptual project exploring emotional boundaries and personal triggers through a button metaphor. It was the first project made together with Hannah Rampitsch, my now business partner at CrossDot Studio, and was completed across the month of December under considerable time pressure. The work visualises how external stimuli - stress, conflict, or social pressure - activate emotional responses, and investigates the tension between reaction and restraint.

02 — Technical Process

Process

The core challenge of this project was establishing a USD Micropipeline for a two-person team, allowing Hannah and I to split work cleanly across lighting, modelling, and materials without stepping on each other. My focus was on modelling, layout, shading, and camera movements, as well as getting USD to behave exactly the way we needed it to. Sound design and editing rounded out my contributions on the post side.

§ USD Micropipeline
Building a Two-Person Pipeline from Scratch

Setting up a USD-based pipeline for two people meant defining clear asset structures, layer composition, and handoff points so that Hannah could work on lighting independently while I handled geometry, shading, and camera without conflicts. Getting this to actually work smoothly, especially within the time constraints of a single December month, was the most technically demanding and rewarding part of the project.
